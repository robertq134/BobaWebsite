Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Besties Boba & Bites ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with our website and our AI chat assistant, Mochi. By using our website or chatting with Mochi, you agree to the practices described here.

The Chat Assistant (Mochi)

Mochi is an automated AI assistant, not a human. It is provided to answer questions about our menu, specials, catering, and hours. Mochi can make mistakes and may occasionally provide inaccurate information — please confirm prices, availability, and other important details directly with the shop before relying on them.

The information Mochi provides is for general informational purposes only and does not constitute professional advice of any kind. Using the chat does not create any professional, advisory, or contractual relationship between you and Besties Boba & Bites, and nothing Mochi says should be treated as a binding offer.

Please do not use the chat to report emergencies or time sensitive issues, and please do not share sensitive personal information (such as payment card numbers, passwords, or government ID numbers) in the chat.

What We Collect

When you use the chat, we process the messages you type in order to generate a response. We do not require you to provide your name, email, or other identifying information to use the chat.

  • Chat messages: the text you send to Mochi.
  • Basic technical data: standard information such as your general request origin, used only to keep the service running and to prevent abuse (for example, rate limiting).

How Your Messages Are Processed

To generate responses, your messages are sent to third party service providers that operate the underlying AI and hosting infrastructure, including OpenAI (which provides the language model) and our hosting and automation providers. These providers may retain message data for a limited period for security and abuse monitoring purposes — in OpenAI's case, generally up to approximately 30 days — after which it is deleted in accordance with their policies. Your messages are not used to train these providers' AI models by default under their standard API terms. You can review OpenAI's data handling practices in their enterprise privacy policy.

How We Use Information

  • To answer your questions and operate the chat.
  • To review conversations in order to improve the quality and accuracy of the service.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of our website.

What We Don't Do

We do not sell your information. We do not use the chat to build advertising profiles about you.

Your Choices

Using the chat is entirely optional. If you prefer not to have your messages processed as described above, you can simply avoid using the chat and instead contact us directly by phone or email.
