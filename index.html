About Us

At Besties Boba & Bites, we believe every sip tastes better with friends! Born from the idea that boba brings people together, we're your local hangout for fun drinks, sweet treats, yummy snacks, and good vibes. Whether you're catching up with your bestie, treating yourself to something tasty, or discovering your new favorite flavor, we've got you covered.

"Come for the boba, stay for the laughter — because everything's better with Besties."
